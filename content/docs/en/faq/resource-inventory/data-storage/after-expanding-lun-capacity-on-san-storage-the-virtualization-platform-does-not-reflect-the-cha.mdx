---
title: "After expanding LUN capacity on SAN storage, the virtualization platform does not reflect the change. How do I resolve this?"
---

## Solution
1.  Try the following approaches:
2.  Go to **Resource Center** &gt; **Data Storage**. Select the Data Center. On the Storage Targets tab of the Data Center details page, select the iSCSI storage and open its details page. Click **More Actions** &gt; **Sync Data**.

3.  Verify that the LUN capacity is now displayed correctly.
