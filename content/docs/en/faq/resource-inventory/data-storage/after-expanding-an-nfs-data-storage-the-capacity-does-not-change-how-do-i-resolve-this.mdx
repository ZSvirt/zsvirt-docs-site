---
title: "After expanding an NFS Data Storage, the capacity does not change. How do I resolve this?"
---

## Symptom
After expanding a Data Storage, the capacity shown on the page remains unchanged.

## Cause
After expanding a Data Storage, a reconnection is required for the new capacity to take effect.

## Solution
1.  In the ZSvirt main menu, go to **Resource Center** &gt; **Data Storage**. On the Data Storage page, click **Actions** next to the expanded Data Storage and select **Reconnect**. The expanded capacity takes effect after the Data Storage reconnects.
