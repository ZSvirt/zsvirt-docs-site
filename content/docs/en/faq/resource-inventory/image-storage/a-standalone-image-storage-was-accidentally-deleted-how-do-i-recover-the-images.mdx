---
title: "A Standalone Image Storage was accidentally deleted. How do I recover the images?"
---

## Solution
1.  Go to **Resource Center** &gt; **Images and Templates**. Select the Data Center and click **Actions** &gt; **Add Image Storage** &gt; **Standalone Image Storage**. Fill in the details of the deleted Image Storage.

2.  Enable the **Retrieve Existing Images** toggle and complete the addition.

3.  Go to **Resource Center** &gt; **Images and Templates** to open the Images interface and verify that the images have been recovered.
