---
title: "Image Storage connection status is abnormal. How do I troubleshoot?"
---

## Solution
-   Try the following troubleshooting steps:
-   If the Management Node was restarted or the virtualization platform was upgraded, wait for the Image Storage to reconnect successfully.

-   Verify that the Image Storage operating system is the customized OS version.

-   Verify that the Image Storage and the Management Node can communicate bidirectionally.
