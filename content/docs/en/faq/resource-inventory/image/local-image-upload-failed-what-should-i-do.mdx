---
title: "Local image upload failed. What should I do?"
---

Local image upload failures are typically caused by one of two reasons. Identify the cause and follow the corresponding solution to troubleshoot.

## Cause 1
The Image Storage is abnormal due to a missing qemu-img package.

## Solution
-   Log in to the virtualization platform, click **Resource Inventory** &gt; **Images and Templates**, select the Image Storage, and click **Actions** &gt; **Reconnect** to reconnect the Image Storage.

## Cause 2
Browser compatibility issue.

## Solution
-   Use a different browser to upload the image. Google Chrome 62 or later is recommended.
