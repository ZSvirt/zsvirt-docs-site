---
title: 'Attach/Detach a Tag'
---

After you create tags, you can attach them to resources or detach them from resources.

1.  (Optional) Attach/detach tags from the resource perspective.

    1.  In the navigation pane, choose **Inventory** &gt; **VM and Host/Bare Metal Management**.

    2.  Select a target host/virtual machine/bare metal instance from the resource tree.

    3.  On the resource details page, click **Actions** &gt; **Tag and Attribute** &gt; **Tag Management**.

    4.  In the Tag Management dialog, add or remove tags as needed.

    5.  Click **OK**.

2.  (Optional) Attach/detach tags from the tag perspective.

    1.  In the navigation pane, choose **O&M Management** &gt; **Tag**.

    2.  On the Tag page, click the name of a tag to enter its details page.

    3.  Click the **Associated Resource** tab.

    4.  On the Associated Resource tab, select resources to attach/detach, and then click **Attach**/**Detach**.
