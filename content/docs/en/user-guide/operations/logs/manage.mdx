---
title: 'Manage Collected Logs'
---

1.  In the navigation pane, choose **O&M Management** &gt; **Log Collection**.

2.  On the Log Collection page:

    1.  Click **Download** to download the collected logs.

    2.  Click **Delete** to delete the collected logs.

    3.  Click **Delete All Logs** to delete all collected logs with one click.
