---
title: 'Delete a Bare Metal Cluster'
---

1.  In the navigation pane, choose **Inventory** &gt; **Bare Metal Management**.

2.  Select the target bare metal cluster.

3.  On the bare metal cluster details page, click **Actions** &gt; **Delete**.

<Callout type="warning">
Deleting bare metal clusters will delete all bare metal chassis and bare metal instances in the cluster. Proceed with caution.
</Callout>
