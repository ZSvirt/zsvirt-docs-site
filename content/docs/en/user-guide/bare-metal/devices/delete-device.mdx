---
title: 'Delete a Bare Metal Chassis'
---

1.  In the navigation pane, choose **Inventory** &gt; **Bare Metal Management**.

2.  Select the target bare metal chassis.

3.  On the bare metal chassis details page, click **Actions** &gt; **Delete**.

<Callout type="info">
Deleting bare metal chassis will also delete bare metal instances created from these bare metal chassis. Proceed with caution.
</Callout>
