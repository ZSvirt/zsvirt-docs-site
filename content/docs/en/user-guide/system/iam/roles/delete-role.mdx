---
title: 'Delete Role'
---

-   Make sure ZSvirt is installed with a valid Advanced Edition license.
-   The selected role is not a system default role.
-   The selected role has been detached from its associated users or user groups.

1.  In the navigation pane, choose **System Management** &gt; **Role**.

2.  On the Role page, select the target role and then click **Actions** &gt; **Delete**.

3.  Review the selected items and click **OK**.
