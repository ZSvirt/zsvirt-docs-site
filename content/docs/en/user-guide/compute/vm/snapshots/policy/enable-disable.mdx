---
title: 'Enable/Disable Snapshot Policy'
---

You can flexibly manage the snapshot policy status, including enabling and disabling. After disabled, the system will pause periodic automatic snapshots until you re-enable the policy.

1.  In the navigation pane, choose **Data Protection** &gt; **Snapshot Policy**.

2.  On the Snapshot Policy page, select the target snapshot and click **Actions** &gt; **Enable/Disable**.
